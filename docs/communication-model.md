# Communication Model

Communication should make initiative state obvious without forcing stakeholders to chase the PM.

## Default Status Format

Every status update should answer:

- Where are we now?
- What changed since the last update?
- What risks or blockers exist?
- What decisions are needed?
- What happens next, by when, and who owns it?

## Cadence

| Moment | Audience | Channel | Owner |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Intake accepted | Requester, business sponsor, PM lead | Initiative update | PM |
| Stage change | Affected stakeholders | Stage-change update | PM |
| Weekly active work | Core team and stakeholders | Weekly status | PM |
| Blocker or material risk | Owner group and escalation path | Risk update | PM |
| Build commitment | Product, Engineering, Design, Business | Readiness summary | PM |
| Launch readiness | Product, Engineering, GTM, Support, Business | Launch readiness update | PM and GTM owner |
| Post-launch review | Core team and stakeholders | Outcome summary | PM |

## Escalation Standard

Escalations should include:

- The decision or blocker.
- Why it matters.
- Options considered.
- Recommended path.
- Consequences of waiting.
- Decision owner and needed date.

Escalation is not failure. It is part of owning the problem when authority, alignment, or priority needs to move.
