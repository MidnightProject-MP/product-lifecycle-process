# Ownership Model

Every initiative must have clear ownership before it moves beyond Intake.

## Required Owners

| Role | Accountable For |
| --- | --- |
| Product Owner | Problem definition, prioritization, requirements, lifecycle stage, stakeholder communication, and product decisions. |
| Engineering Owner | Technical approach, feasibility, delivery risks, implementation quality, and technical tradeoffs. |
| Business Sponsor | Business outcome, priority context, operational constraints, and acceptance of business tradeoffs. |

## Conditional Owners

| Role | Needed When | Accountable For |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Design Owner | User experience or workflow change is material. | UX direction, usability, interaction quality, and design readiness. |
| GTM Owner | Customer-facing launch, positioning, packaging, pricing, or sales impact exists. | Launch messaging, enablement, market readiness, and adoption support. |
| Support Owner | Support process, documentation, training, or customer escalation impact exists. | Support readiness, escalation handling, and customer-facing help content. |
| Legal or Compliance Owner | Legal, regulatory, privacy, security, or contractual risk exists. | Review, approval, and risk guidance. |
| Executive Sponsor | Strategic priority, major tradeoff, or cross-functional conflict exists. | Final escalation, prioritization, and risk acceptance. |

## Ownership Standards

- One PM owns the initiative end to end.
- Each blocker has a named owner and target resolution date.
- Decisions are recorded with owner, date, rationale, and impact.
- Ownership does not require doing every task; it requires driving clarity, follow-through, and communication.
